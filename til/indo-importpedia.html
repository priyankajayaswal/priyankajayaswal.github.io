Services Imports

Composition

Total services imports ~$188.7 Bn dominated by Business Services (consulting, R&D, advertising) and Transport (Indian importers paying foreign shipping/airline freight). Travel imports grew strongly post-pandemic. The net services surplus (~$199 Bn) cushions the overall trade deficit.

Top Import Source Countries

Supplier Concentration

China dominates (~$113 Bn) — electronics, APIs, solar, machinery. Russia #2 on discounted crude post-2022. UAE/Saudi/Iraq supply the bulk of crude/LPG. USA is the only top-5 partner where India runs a goods surplus (~$41 Bn).

India runs a ~$99 Bn bilateral deficit with China alone — the largest single imbalance. PLI + 'China + 1' aim to reduce this in electronics, APIs, capital goods.
