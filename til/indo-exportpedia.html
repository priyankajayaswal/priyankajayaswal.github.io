Services Exports — Sector-by-Sector

Services Composition

India's services exports are IT-heavy: software services alone (RBI survey) clocked $204.7 Bn, of which IT Services contributed ~$131 Bn and BPO/ITES ~$58 Bn. Including foreign affiliate sales the IT footprint exceeds $218 Bn; MeitY's parliamentary estimate puts it at $224.4 Bn.

The fastest-growing segment is Business Services — driven by India's 1,700+ Global Capability Centres (GCCs) exporting R&D, consulting, design, and analytics.

USA accounts for ~53% of services exports; Europe ~33% (growing faster than the US at +14.3%). 90.7% of software services are delivered off-site.

Top Export Destination Countries

Geographic Concentration

The top 10 countries absorb ~53% of India's merchandise exports. The US is dominant (~20%). The UAE serves as both an end market and a re-export hub to GCC & Africa, supported by the India-UAE CEPA. The Netherlands has risen rapidly as a European gateway, primarily for refined petroleum.

Emerging markets — Bangladesh, Singapore, Saudi Arabia — diversify risk, while China remains a small export market despite being India's largest import source.
