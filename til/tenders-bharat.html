📚 Citations & Methodology refs
Every tender card on this page carries an inline citation chip (e.g. [CPPP], [IREPS]) linking to the official portal where the bid is published. Click Open ↗ on any card to land on the original notice. The methodology, data lineage and a full reference list are below.

🧪 Methodology

  1. Discovery: daily crawl of CPPP, GeM, IREPS, Defence DPP and all 28 state & 8 UT e-procurement portals.
  2. Normalisation: bids are mapped to a common schema — jurisdiction, region, district, sector, value (₹ Cr), publish & close dates.
  3. Geo-tagging: district is parsed from tender title/body; "Pan-India" used for nationwide procurements.
  4. Sectoral tagging: 15 sectors aligned with NIC + DPIIT taxonomy.
  5. Quality flags: MSME-friendly, ₹100 Cr+, green/renewable, closing-this-week.
  6. Citation: every record retains the source-portal URL; the page links straight back rather than republishing the notice.

Last refresh: 2026-05-24 · sample shown is illustrative; production deployment hooks into live source APIs.
