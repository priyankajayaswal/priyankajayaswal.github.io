Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Centre → State → District → Block/Autonomous Council → GP/Ward

Assam's chain is not one uniform panchayat ladder. In normal rural areas it runs through Zilla Parishad, Anchalik Panchayat and Gram Panchayat. In Sixth Schedule tribal areas, autonomous councils exercise the local-government space instead of the regular Panchayati Raj system.

Reading it: Outside autonomous areas, the local chain is block → GP → ward. Inside BTC, Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao, the Sixth Schedule council is the local democratic layer; regular PRI counts should not be added there.

🛠 How this catalogue works

Assam is catalogued with the same Centre → State → District pattern as other state pages, with one important exception: Sixth Schedule autonomous councils replace the ordinary panchayat system in tribal areas.

① The 7-tier pyramid

Named inline: Union ministers, MPs, the state executive, Assembly officers, district list and statutory heads. Linked out: live rosters for DC/SP postings, judges, GP wards and council-member registers.

Counts used here: 14 Lok Sabha seats, 7 Rajya Sabha seats, 126 Assembly seats, 35 districts, 219 blocks and about 2,197 GPs.
