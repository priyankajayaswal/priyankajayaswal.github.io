Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Centre → State → District → Block → GP → Ward

Six tiers. Top two are elected to Parliament/Assembly; bottom four mix elected representatives with civil-service counterparts. Every citizen sits inside one chain that touches all six.

Reading it: Every Gujarati citizen has, simultaneously, a Panch/Ward Member, Sarpanch, Taluka Panchayat member, ZP member, MLA, MP (LS), MP (RS) and a CM — eight elected layers, plus the appointed Collector/SP/TDO chain. This catalogue names them where it can, links the live registers where the count is too large.

🛠 How this catalogue works

A visual walkthrough of the 7-tier governance pyramid this site catalogues, and the rule deciding what gets named inline vs linked to a live register.

① The 7-tier pyramid

Gujarat's governance stack runs from 1 Union Cabinet at the apex down to 14,503+ Gram Panchayats and ward registers at the base. Each tier roughly multiplies the count below it by 10×–100×.

Cards in every lens use the same colour key as the pyramid: 🟢 named inline · 🟠 linked to live register · 🔴 too volatile to embed.

📍 District Administration — 33 Districts

Each district is headed administratively by a Collector/District Magistrate, with police led by an SP or Commissionerate as applicable. Postings are transfer-sensitive, so officer cells link to live official rosters.

Collector/SP names are intentionally linked out rather than frozen: Gujarat transfer orders can change district heads at short notice. District count is fixed here at 33.
