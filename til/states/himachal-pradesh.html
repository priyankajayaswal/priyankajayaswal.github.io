Himachal Pradesh's compact elected stack fits the top-level catalogue: 7 MPs, 68 MLAs and key state officials inline; roughly 3,615 gram panchayats and ward rosters link to live registers.

🛠 How this catalogue works

The page uses the same rule for each layer: inline stable, small rosters; link out large or fast-changing rosters.

? The Himachal Pradesh scale

Seven MPs, 68 MLAs, the cabinet, district list and statutory bodies fit in one audited HTML page.

About 3,615 gram panchayats and roughly 21,000 ward offices are better served by LGD, eGramSwaraj, SEC and district portals.

Inline vs link-out decision
The promise: every named person on this page has a source trail; layers not named inline hand off to authoritative live registers.

Recommendation

Hybrid page is the right unit. This HTML can keep the top elected and appointed offices auditable while delegating gram-panchayat and ward rosters to LGD, eGramSwaraj, SEC and district portals.

Suggested next iterations

  1. Per-district pages ? 12 small pages, each listing DC/SP/SDM/BDO links and ZP details.
  2. GP roster sync ? fetch LGD and SEC data nightly rather than freezing 3,615 names in HTML.
