🔗 Centre → State → District → Taluk → GP → Ward

Karnataka is bicameral at the legislature layer and three-tier at the rural local-body layer. Large, volatile GP/ward rosters link to official live registers.

🛠 How this catalogue works

Karnataka uses the standard 8-lens template and exercises the Vidhan Parishad-only path.

Inline vs link-out

Compact layers (Union ministers, MPs, cabinet, district list, top statutory bodies) are named inline. MLAs/MLCs are tallied with official rosters linked. Gram panchayat and ward layers link to LGD, SEC Karnataka and eGramSwaraj.

Scale check

MPs 40 · Cabinet 32 · Legislature 299 · Districts 31 · Taluks 236 · GPs 5,977 · wards ≈92k.

Traceability rule: every named person has a source within one click; bulk rosters are delegated to authoritative live systems.
