Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Centre → State → District → ADC → Village Council

Mizoram's citizen-facing chain is small at the parliamentary layer, compact at the Assembly layer, and distinctive at the local layer: Village Councils are the base, while CADC, LADC and MADC add Sixth Schedule autonomy in Chakma, Lai and Mara areas.

Key adaptation: this page intentionally does not use ZP / Panchayat Samiti / Gram Panchayat labels for Mizoram.

🛠 How this catalogue works

The page names compact layers inline and links volatile or very large rosters to the official live register.

① Mizoram-specific pyramid

Inline: CM/Governor, ministry, MPs, Assembly party math, office-bearers, 11 districts, and the three ADC executive heads/status.

Link out: DC/SP transfer rosters, court sitting lists, Village Council member rolls, and statutory-body live member pages.

② Decision rule

  • Inline if the layer has fewer than about 50 stable names.
  • Hybrid for 50-500 nodes: show totals and link the roster.
  • Link out for ~834 Village Councils and thousands of members.

⚖ Judiciary — Gauhati High Court, Aizawl Bench

Mizoram is under the Gauhati High Court. The Aizawl Bench is a permanent Bench established on 5-Jul-1990 and exercises appellate jurisdiction over Mizoram courts, including District Council Courts in the Sixth Schedule areas.

🏞 Local Self-Government — Village Councils + Sixth Schedule ADCs

Mizoram is not a standard ZP/Panchayat Samiti/Gram Panchayat state. The base elected rural institution is the Village Council; the Chakma, Lai and Mara areas also sit under Sixth Schedule Autonomous District Councils.
