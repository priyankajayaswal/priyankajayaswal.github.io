Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Centre → Administrator → District → Dweep Panchayat → Ward

Lakshadweep is governed under the Union Territory model: the Administrator runs the administration under the President/Union Government, while local democratic representation enters through Parliament and the Panchayat system.

Reading it: A resident has one Lok Sabha MP, no MLA/MLC layer, one Administrator-led UT administration, one district administration, a Dweep Panchayat and District Panchayat representation.

🛠 How this Lakshadweep catalogue works

This page follows the same Bharat Sanchar shell as the state pages, but adapts the governance stack for a Union Territory without a legislature.

UT model, not state model

There is no Chief Minister, Cabinet, Vidhan Sabha or Vidhan Parishad. The Administrator is the apex executive, Parliament provides national representation, and elected local government flows through Dweep Panchayats plus one District Panchayat.

Inline rows are used only for stable, small rosters; volatile postings link to official live directories.

🌾 Panchayati Raj — Dweep Panchayats + District Panchayat

Lakshadweep's local-government layer is created by the Lakshadweep Panchayats Regulation, 1994: one Village (Dweep) Panchayat for each inhabited island and one District Panchayat for the whole UT.
