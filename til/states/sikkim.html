🛠 How this Sikkim catalogue works

The page names compact, stable offices and links to official live rosters where postings or local-office holders change frequently.

Inline vs live register

Sikkim is small enough to inline all MPs, the Council of Ministers, Assembly party tally, apex officers and six districts. GP/ward-level representatives still belong in RMDD, LGD and eGramSwaraj because local rosters are transfer/election sensitive.

Recommendation

One page is feasible for the top stack. Sikkim's state/Union layer is compact, so this HTML can credibly name the MPs, cabinet and HC judges. Panchayat and ward rosters still belong in LGD/eGramSwaraj/RMDD live registers.

Suggested next iterations

  1. Per-district drilldowns — six small pages for DC/SP/SDM/BAC links and district-specific schemes.
  2. Article 371F explainer — standalone note connecting Sangha, reserved seats and Sikkim's merger provisions.
  3. Register sync — pull LGD/eGramSwaraj IDs for all 199 GPUs into machine-readable JSON.
