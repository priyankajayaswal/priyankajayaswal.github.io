Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Union/LG → Elected UT Government → District/Region → Local Body → Ward

Puducherry is smaller than Delhi but structurally similar as a UT-with-legislature. Article 239A gives an elected Assembly and ministry, while the LG remains the Union's administrator and exercises statutory discretion where law requires.

Reading it: the same UT government administers four separated enclaves: Puducherry and Karaikal in Tamil Nadu surroundings, Mahe in Kerala, and Yanam in Andhra Pradesh.

① The Puducherry stack

Puducherry has a 33-member Legislative Assembly under Article 239A, a Chief Minister and Council of Ministers, and a Lieutenant Governor. Its geography is unusual: four small enclaves are administered as one UT.

Cards use the same colour key as other state pages: 🟢 named inline · 🟠 tally + link · 🔴 live register only.

② Research rule

Inline small stable rosters (MPs, CM/LG, ministers, office-bearers, districts). Link out live rosters where transfers/elections make names volatile (all MLAs, local councillors, line officers).

③ Special UT cautions

Do not read Puducherry like an ordinary state. The Assembly exists under Article 239A; the LG can reserve matters for the President and the Union retains a stronger channel than in states. Unlike Delhi's Article 239AA text, Puducherry does not have the same explicit Land/Police/Public Order carve-out, but central control and LG discretion have repeatedly mattered in practice.

📍 District Administration — 4 non-contiguous districts

Puducherry is one UT made of four separated former French settlements: Puducherry and Karaikal are surrounded by Tamil Nadu, Mahe by Kerala, and Yanam by Andhra Pradesh.

Non-contiguous geography: field administration cannot be read as one contiguous map. Mahe and Yanam are far from Puducherry/Karaikal and rely on regional administrators plus UT-wide departments.
