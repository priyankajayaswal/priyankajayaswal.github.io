Centre → State → District → Block → GP → Ward

Jharkhand's chain has the usual Union/state/district tiers plus a strong Scheduled Areas layer: the Fifth Schedule and PESA shape Gram Sabha consent, natural-resource decisions and local customary institutions across most rural Jharkhand.

How this catalogue works

A point-in-time, source-linked catalogue: inline where the roster is tractable; live-register links where names are numerous or postings change quickly.

Decision rule: Union ministers, MPs, the council of ministers, party tallies and constitutional offices are named inline. MLAs are tallied and linked to the Assembly roster. District, block, GP and ward records link to official district, LGD, SEC and eGramSwaraj registers.

Verification date: 24-May-2026. If an official live roster conflicts with this static page, the official roster wins.

Scheduled Areas note: Most rural Jharkhand is under the Fifth Schedule; PESA is therefore a first-order governance fact, not a footnote.
