Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Centre/LG → NCT Cabinet → District → Urban local body → Ward

Delhi's chain has two parallel tracks: elected NCT institutions for transferred subjects, and Union/LG control over police, public order and land.

Reading it: Every Delhi resident has an MP, an MLA, and usually an MCD councillor, but police, public order and land run through the Union/LG track under Article 239AA.

🛠 How this catalogue works

A visual walkthrough of the Delhi NCT governance stack this site catalogues, and the rule deciding what gets named inline vs linked to a live register.

① The Delhi NCT stack

Delhi's governance stack is not a normal state pyramid: the Union/LG track controls police, public order and land, while the elected NCT government runs devolved departments and urban services are split across MCD, NDMC and the Cantonment Board.

Cards in every lens use the same colour key as the pyramid: 🟢 named inline · 🟠 linked to live register · 🔴 too volatile to embed.

Delhi is a UT with a legislature, not a state. Article 239AA gives the Assembly power over State List / Concurrent List subjects only so far as they apply to a Union Territory, and specifically excludes entries 1, 2 and 18 of the State List: public order, police and land. That is why the LG/Union channel remains central to Delhi governance even after an Assembly election.
Why not list all 70 MLAs inline? The Assembly roster is tractable, but the official member directory is the source of record for photos, committees, contact changes and disqualification/bye-election updates. This page uses the party tally and office-bearers inline, then links the live register.

Delhi deviation from the Delhi template: the PRI lens is deliberately replaced with Urban Local Bodies. Treating Delhi as a normal panchayat-bearing state would invent non-existent GP/block layers and hide the Centre-vs-NCT split that is central to Delhi governance.
  1. Inline small, stable layers — MPs, cabinet, Assembly officers, DMs and statutory bodies are named or linked directly.
  2. Link volatile rosters — judges, MLAs, councillors and district postings change; official live registers remain authoritative.
  3. Make constitutional exceptions explicit — Article 239AA and the Union-controlled police/public-order/land track are called out in the Executive, Legislature and Scope lenses.
