🛠 How this catalogue works

Manipur is treated as a point-in-time snapshot: named top-level offices inline; volatile local rosters link to the official live register.

① The 2026 governance stack

The normal chain is Union → State → district → local body. Under President's Rule the State executive layer is Governor-led, while the Assembly is retained in suspended animation.

Local democracy is bifurcated: valley districts use Panchayati Raj institutions; hill areas use Autonomous District Councils under the Manipur Hill Areas District Councils Act.

🏛 State Executive — President's Rule (Article 356)

There is no Council of Ministers while President's Rule is in force. Administration runs through the Governor and the state civil service on behalf of the President of India.

Recommendation

Hybrid is the only honest answer for Manipur. The top layer is small enough to name. Below district level, conflict conditions, by-elections, postings and ADC/GP roster churn make live official registers safer than embedded static names.
