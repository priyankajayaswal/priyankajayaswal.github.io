🔗 Union/LG track + elected UT track

Public Order and Police are retained by the LG/Union channel, while the elected ministry runs transferred departments. This mirrors Delhi's UT-with-Assembly treatment but is newer and constitutionally novel for J&K.

🛠 How this catalogue works

Small stable layers are named inline; volatile or very large rosters link to official live registers.

Reserved subjects: Public Order and Police are LG/Union jurisdiction, not the elected CM's jurisdiction.
Post-2019 context: Article 370/35A abrogation and the Reorganisation Act converted the former state into the UTs of J&K and Ladakh; J&K later restored an elected Assembly.
