Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Centre → State → District → Block → GP → Ward

Six tiers. Top two are elected to Parliament/Assembly; bottom four mix elected representatives with civil-service counterparts. Every citizen sits inside one chain that touches all six.

Reading it: Every Andhra Pradesh citizen has, simultaneously, a ward member, Sarpanch, MPP/ZP representatives, MLA, MLC layer, MP (LS), MP (RS) and a CM — plus the appointed Collector/SP/MPDO chain. This catalogue names the tractable top layer and links live registers where the count is too large.

🛠 How this catalogue works

A visual walkthrough of the 7-tier governance pyramid this site catalogues, and the rule deciding what gets named inline vs linked to a live register.

① The 7-tier pyramid

Andhra Pradesh's governance stack runs from 1 Union Cabinet at the apex down to ~1.34 lakh ward members at the base. Each tier multiplies the count below it by 10×–100×.

Cards in every lens use the same colour key as the pyramid: 🟢 named inline · 🟠 linked to live register · 🔴 too volatile to embed.

🏛 State Executive — N. Chandrababu Naidu Ministry (2024 →)

NDA coalition government led by TDP, with JSP and BJP ministers. Snapshot verified for 24-May-2026; portfolios can change by G.O.

Three-capital arrangement. The 2019–2024 policy framed Amaravati as legislative capital, Visakhapatnam as executive capital, and Kurnool as judicial capital. The arrangement remains legally/politically pending in this snapshot; the High Court continues at Amaravati and the government has restarted Amaravati capital works. This page therefore uses Amaravati for the judiciary and records the three-capital plan as a pending governance flag.
