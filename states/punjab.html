Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Centre → State → District → Block → GP → Ward

Punjab's stack runs from Parliament and the State Assembly down to district, block, Gram Panchayat and ward institutions.

Reading it: Every Punjabi citizen has a ward member/panch, sarpanch, Panchayat Samiti/ZP layer, MLA, MP (LS), MP (RS) and a CM, plus the appointed DC/SSP/BDO chain.

🛠 How this catalogue works

A visual walkthrough of Punjab's governance pyramid and the rule deciding what gets named inline vs linked to a live register.

① The 7-tier pyramid

Punjab's governance stack runs from 20 Parliament seats and 117 Assembly seats down to roughly 13,241 Gram Panchayats and ward-level representatives.

Cards in every lens use the same rule: 🟢 named inline · 🟠 aggregate + link · 🔴 too large/volatile to embed.

Punjab governance pyramid (log scale)

② The decision flow — inline or link?

The rule: MPs, ministers, Assembly party tally, apex officers, districts and statutory bodies are inline. Gram Panchayat and ward rosters are linked to official live registers because the node count is too large for one HTML file.

③ Verification date

Snapshot verified: 24-May-2026. Roster links are preferred where posts are high-turnover.
