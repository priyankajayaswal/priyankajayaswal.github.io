Relmap ? how the chain connects

🔗 Centre → State → District → Block → GP → Ward

Six tiers. Top two are elected to Parliament/Assembly; bottom four mix elected representatives with civil-service counterparts. Every citizen sits inside one chain that touches all six.

Reading it: Every Haryana citizen has a Ward Member/Panch, Sarpanch, Panchayat Samiti representative, ZP member, MLA, MP (LS), MP (RS) and a CM, plus the appointed DC/SP/BDO chain. This catalogue names them where it can and links live registers where the count is too large.

🛠 How this catalogue works

A visual walkthrough of the governance pyramid and the rule deciding what gets named inline vs linked to a live register.

? The 7-tier pyramid

Haryana's governance stack runs from Union representation at the apex down to thousands of Gram Panchayats at the base. Each tier multiplies the count below it by 10–10×.

Cards in every lens use the same rule: 🟢 named inline · 🟡 aggregates plus links · 🔴 live register only.

7-tier governance pyramid (log scale)

? The decision flow ? inline or link?

Inline ministers, MPs, district rows and assembly tallies. Hybrid MLA and block layers. Link out Gram Panchayat and ward rosters because LGD/eGramSwaraj are live and authoritative.
The promise: every named person on this page comes with a link back to an official or primary source. Every layer this page does not name comes with a link to the authoritative live register that does.
