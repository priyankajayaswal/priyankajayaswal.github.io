Andaman & Nicobar Islands (UT) Governance Chain — at a Glance

A Union Territory without a legislature: no Chief Minister, no Council of Ministers, no Vidhan Sabha, no Vidhan Parishad. The Lieutenant Governor and UT Administration run the executive chain, with local self-government split between Andaman panchayats and Nicobar tribal councils.

UT without legislature. As of 24-May-2026, Andaman & Nicobar Islands is administered by the President through the Lieutenant Governor, Admiral D. K. Joshi. There is no elected UT cabinet or assembly; Parliament representation is 1 Lok Sabha seat and 0 Rajya Sabha seats.

Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Union → LG Administration → District → Andaman PRI / Nicobar Tribal Councils

The island chain sits at the mouth of the Malacca Strait in the Indo-Pacific. The governance model is deliberately compact: Union control and the LG Administration sit above district administration, with democratic panchayats in Andaman and customary tribal councils in Nicobar.

Reading it: no CM or MLA tier appears because the UT has no legislature. The Nicobar branch is deliberately separate because Tribal Councils govern under protected-area/customary arrangements rather than the standard Gram Panchayat model.

🗳 Legislature — Not Applicable

Andaman & Nicobar Islands has no Vidhan Sabha and no Vidhan Parishad. Legislative authority for the UT is exercised through Parliament and Union Territory administration under the constitutional UT framework.

📍 District Administration — 3 Districts

The UT has three districts: South Andaman, North & Middle Andaman, and Nicobar. Sri Vijaya Puram is the capital and district headquarters for South Andaman.

District officer postings rotate by Administration orders. The table therefore records headquarters and functions inline, and links official district / administration directories for live Deputy Commissioner and police rosters.
