Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Centre → State → District → Block → GP → Ward

Six tiers. Top two are elected to Parliament/Assembly; bottom four mix elected representatives with civil-service counterparts. Every citizen sits inside one chain that touches all six.

Reading it: Every Uttar Pradeshi citizen has, simultaneously, a Ward Member, Mukhiya, Pramukh, ZP member, MLA, MLC, MP (LS), MP (RS) and a CM — nine elected layers, plus the appointed DM/SP/BDO chain. This catalogue names them where it can, links the live registers where the count is too large.

🛠 How this catalogue works

A visual walkthrough of the 7-tier governance pyramid this site catalogues, and the rule deciding what gets named inline vs linked to a live register.

① The 7-tier pyramid

Uttar Pradesh's governance stack runs from 1 Union Cabinet at the apex down to 1.15 lakh Ward Members at the base. Each tier roughly multiplies the count below it by 10×–100×.

Cards in every lens use the same colour key as the pyramid: 🟢 named inline · 🟠 linked to live register · 🔴 too volatile to embed.

📐 Scope memo — why some rosters are inline and others link out

UP is the stress test: 80 LS seats, 31 RS seats, 403 MLAs, 100 MLCs and roughly 58,189 Gram Panchayats.
