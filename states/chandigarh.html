UT without legislature: Chandigarh has no Chief Minister, Cabinet, Vidhan Sabha or Vidhan Parishad. It is administered directly by an Administrator appointed under the Union Territory framework; by convention, that Administrator is the Governor of Punjab.

Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Union → Administrator → District → Municipal Corporation → Ward

Chandigarh is simultaneously a Union Territory, the capital of Punjab, and the capital of Haryana. The elected civic layer is municipal, not legislative.

🛠 How this catalogue works

Chandigarh uses a Ladakh-style deviation from the state-page template: the Chief Minister, Cabinet and Legislature lenses are replaced by the Administrator and Municipal Corporation lenses.

① The Chandigarh stack

The constitutional chain is Union → Administrator → UT Administration. Article 240 permits the President to make regulations for several Union Territories, including Chandigarh. Local electoral accountability is primarily through the Lok Sabha seat and the 35-ward Municipal Corporation.

The page therefore omits CM, Cabinet, Vidhan Sabha and Vidhan Parishad rosters instead of showing empty state-style tables.

Chandigarh layers
Deviation report: all state-only branches are intentionally absent. The Municipal Corporation and Mayor are the civic substitute for the legislature / panchayat slots, while the Administrator lens carries executive authority.
