Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Centre → State → District → Block → GP → Ward

Six tiers. Top two are elected to Parliament/Assembly; bottom four mix elected representatives with civil-service counterparts. Every citizen sits inside one chain that touches all six.

Reading it: A Tamil Nadu citizen has a Ward Member, Village Panchayat President, Panchayat Union Chairperson, District Panchayat/ULB representative, MLA, MP (LS), MP (RS) and a CM — with no live Vidhan Parishad layer.

🛠 How this catalogue works

A visual walkthrough of the 7-tier governance pyramid this site catalogues, and the rule deciding what gets named inline vs linked to a live register.

① The 7-tier pyramid

Tamil Nadu's governance stack runs from the Union layer down to roughly 99,000 village-panchayat ward members at the base. Each tier roughly multiplies the count below it by 10×–100×.

Cards in every lens use the same colour key as the pyramid: 🟢 named inline · 🟠 linked to live register · 🔴 too volatile to embed.

Recommendation

Hybrid is the only honest answer. This file embeds high-value, tractable lists (MPs, cabinet, Assembly tallies, district index) and delegates all block/GP/ward names to live registers. Tamil Nadu's abolished-since-1986 Vidhan Parishad is recorded as a historical footnote rather than a live layer.

  2. Nightly LGD/eGramSwaraj fetch to refresh block and GP counts.
