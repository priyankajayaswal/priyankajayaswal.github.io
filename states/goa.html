Relmap — how the chain connects

🔗 Centre → State → District → Block → GP → Ward

Six tiers. Top two are elected to Parliament/Assembly; bottom four mix elected representatives with civil-service counterparts. Every citizen sits inside one chain that touches all six.

Reading it: Every Goan citizen has, simultaneously, a Ward Member, Mukhiya, Pramukh, ZP member, MLA, MLC, MP (LS), MP (RS) and a CM — nine elected layers, plus the appointed DM/SP/BDO chain. This catalogue names them where it can, links the live registers where the count is too large.

Recommendation

Goa fits a hybrid page well. The top chain (Union, MPs, Cabinet, Assembly officers, two districts and statutory bodies) is small enough to audit inline. The 191 GP and 1,561 ward-member layer should remain linked to SEC/LGD/eGramSwaraj because by-elections and local vacancies change faster than this static page.

Suggested next iterations

  1. Two district pages — one for North Goa and one for South Goa with live Collectorate and police chains.
  2. PRI scraper — pull SEC/LGD/eGramSwaraj lists into small generated tables, refreshed by election cycle.
  3. Constituency resolver — map village/ward to GP → ZP → Assembly → Lok Sabha.
