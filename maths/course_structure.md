## CATEGORY: Foundations (core undergraduate math)

### 1. MATHEMATICS-I

#### Differential Calculus (Functions of one Variable)
- Rolle's theorem
- Cauchy's mean value theorem (Lagrange's as special case)
- Taylor's and Maclaurin's theorems with remainders
- Indeterminate forms
- Concavity, convexity, points of inflexion
- Asymptotes and curvature

#### Differential Calculus (Functions of several variables)
- Limit, continuity, differentiability of multivariable functions
- Partial derivatives and geometrical interpretation
- Differentials, derivatives of composite and implicit functions
- Higher order derivatives and commutativity
- Euler's theorem on homogeneous functions
- Harmonic functions
- Taylor's expansion (several variables)
- Maxima and minima — Lagrange's method of multipliers

#### Ordinary Differential Equations
- First order: exact, linear, Bernoulli's form
- Second order with constant coefficients
- Method of variation of parameters
- General linear ODEs with constant coefficients
- Euler's equations
- Systems of differential equations

#### Sequences and Series
- Sequences and their limits
- Convergence of series
- Comparison test, Ratio test, Root test
- Absolute and conditional convergence
- Alternating series, Power series

#### Complex Variables
- Limit, continuity, differentiability, analyticity
- Cauchy-Riemann equations
- Line integrals in complex plane
- Cauchy's integral theorem, independence of path
- Cauchy's integral formula
- Derivatives of analytic functions
- Taylor's series, Laurent's series
- Zeros and singularities
- Residue theorem, evaluation of real integrals

---

### 2. MATHEMATICS-II

#### Linear Algebra (introductory)
- Algebra of matrices, vector spaces
- Linear dependence, basis, linear transformations
- Rank and inverse of a matrix
- Solution of algebraic equations, consistency conditions
- Hermitian, skew-Hermitian, unitary matrices
- Bilinear forms, eigenvalues and eigenvectors
- Numerical solution: Gauss, Gauss-Jordan, Gauss-Seidel

#### Integral Calculus
- Fundamental theorem of integral calculus
- Mean value theorems
- Definite integrals, reduction formulae
- Convergence of improper integrals, tests of convergence
- Beta and Gamma functions
- Differentiation under integral sign (Leibnitz rule)
- Rectification, double and triple integrals
- Area, surfaces, volumes
- Change of variables, Jacobians

#### Vector Calculus
- Scalar and vector fields, level surfaces
- Directional derivative, Gradient, Curl, Divergence, Laplacian
- Line and surface integrals
- Theorems of Green, Gauss, Stokes
- Line integrals independent of path

#### Numerical Analysis (introductory)
- Finite differences
- Newton's forward and backward interpolation
- Central difference interpolation
- Trapezoidal and Simpson's 1/3rd rules
- Solution of polynomial and transcendental equations
- Bisection, Newton-Raphson, Regula-Falsi methods

---

### 3. TRANSFORM CALCULUS

#### Laplace Transform
- Definition, linearity property
- Conditions for existence
- First and second shifting properties
- Transform of derivatives and integrals
- Unit step functions, Dirac delta function, error function
- Differentiation and integration of transforms
- Convolution theorem, inversion
- Periodic functions
- Evaluation of integrals by Laplace Transform
- Solution of initial and boundary value problems

#### Fourier Series
- Periodic functions
- Fourier series representation
- Half range series, sine and cosine series
- Fourier integral formula, Parseval's identity

#### Fourier Transform
- Fourier Transform, sine and cosine transforms
- Linearity, scaling, frequency/time shifting
- Self reciprocity, convolution theorem
- Applications to boundary value problems

#### Other Transforms
- Z-Transform (brief introduction)
- Mellin Transform
- Wavelet Transform

---

### 4. PARTIAL DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS

#### Foundations
- Power series solution of ODE review
- Frobenius series
- Bessel functions and Legendre polynomials

#### First Order PDEs
- Linear and quasi-linear equations
- Classification of integrals
- Lagrange's method, geometrical interpretation
- Compatibility condition, Charpit's method
- Special types of first order equations

#### Second Order PDEs
- Constant and variable coefficients
- Classification and reduction to canonical form
- Characteristics
- Cauchy problem, Neumann and Dirichlet problems

#### Solution Methods
- Fourier series solution of wave equation
- Vibrations of a string
- Riemann's method for hyperbolic equations
- Separation of variables (heat, Laplace, diffusion)
- Integral transform method

## CATEGORY: Pure Mathematics (abstract/theoretical)

### 5. LINEAR ALGEBRA (advanced)
- Vector spaces over arbitrary fields
- Linear combination, dependence, independence
- Basis and dimension, inner-product spaces
- Linear transformations, matrix representation
- Linear functionals, dual spaces
- Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, rank, nullity
- Cayley-Hamilton theorem
- Norms of vectors and matrices
- Adjoint of an operator
- Normal, unitary, Hermitian, skew-Hermitian operators
- Quadratic forms

### 6. REAL ANALYSIS
- Dedekind's definition of real numbers
- Field and order axioms
- Countable and uncountable sets
- Supremum, infimum, limit points
- Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem, open and closed sets
- Limit inferior, superior, sequences
- Bounded and monotonic sequences
- Cauchy sequence, general principle of convergence
- Cantor's theorem on nested intervals
- Compact sets, Heine-Borel theorem
- Continuity, uniform continuity
- Differentiability, Taylor's and Maclaurin's theorems
- Riemann integration, conditions for integrability
- Fundamental theorem, mean value theorems
- Improper integrals, absolute/conditional convergence
- Sequences and series of functions, uniform convergence
- Cantor's definition of real numbers
- Metric sets

### 7. MODERN ALGEBRA
- Groups, subgroups, cyclic groups, cosets
- Lagrange's theorem
- Normal subgroup, quotient group
- Homomorphism, fundamental theorem of homomorphism
- Permutation group, Cayley's theorem
- Direct product of groups
- Commutative ring with identity: axioms, examples
- Integral domain, field, ideals
- Quotient ring, prime and maximal ideal
- Principal ideal domain, Euclidean domain
- Field of quotients of an integral domain
- Polynomial ring over a field
- Roots of polynomials, extension of fields, splitting fields

### 8. FUNCTIONAL ANALYSIS
- Metric spaces, complete metric spaces
- Banach contraction mapping theorem
- Banach spaces, bounded linear functionals and operators
- Dual spaces
- Hahn-Banach theorem
- Uniform boundedness principle
- Open mapping and closed graph theorems
- Weak convergence
- Hilbert spaces, orthonormal sets
- Riesz representation theorem
- Bounded linear operators on Hilbert spaces

### 9. MEASURE THEORY AND INTEGRATION
- Prerequisite: Real Analysis
- Algebra of sets, ring, sigma-ring, field, sigma field
- Monotone class
- Lebesgue measure and outer measure
- Measurable sets and functions
- Littlewood's three principles
- Lebesgue integral (bounded, non-negative, general)
- Convergence in measure
- Functions of bounded variation, absolute continuity
- Differentiation and integration
- Signed measure, Hahn-Jordan decomposition
- Radon-Nikodym and Lebesgue decomposition theorems
- Product measures, Fubini's theorem
- Lp spaces, Minkowski and Hölder inequalities

### 10. TOPOLOGY
- Prerequisite: Real Analysis
- Topological spaces, weak topology
- Subspace topology, product and quotient spaces
- Continuous maps and homeomorphism
- Hausdorff spaces
- Compact and locally compact spaces
- Separation axioms
- Connectedness, paths, equivalence classes of paths
- Path connected spaces

### 11. NUMBER THEORY
- Prerequisite: Modern Algebra, Real Analysis
- Peano's axioms, divisibility
- Properties of integers and prime numbers
- Fundamental theorem of arithmetic
- Congruences (degree one and higher)
- Quadratic residues, quadratic reciprocity, Jacobi symbol
- Greatest integer function
- Arithmetic functions, Möbius inversion formula
- Recurrence functions
- Diophantine equations
- Simple continued fractions
- Distribution of primes
- Algebraic numbers and algebraic number fields
- Partition function

- Eigenvalues, eigenfunctions, expansions, completeness

#### Special Functions

- Bessel equation and solution

- Generating function, recurrence relations

#### Stability Theory
- Autonomous systems

- Linear plane autonomous systems

#### Equations of Motion

- Potential flows

#### Three-dimensional Flows
- Singularities and image systems

- Axi-symmetric flows, Stokes stream function

#### Two-dimensional Flows
- Stream function and complex potential
- Irrotational incompressible flows
- Image systems, Milne-Thomson circle theorem

#### Viscous Flows
- Stress analysis, stress-strain relations
- Navier-Stokes equations
- Exact solutions of Navier-Stokes
- Flow past a sphere
- Prandtl boundary layer theory
- Kármán integral equation
- Inviscid compressible flow, pressure propagation

#### Volterra Integral Equations

- Relationship between linear DEs and Volterra equations
- Resolvent kernel
- Method of successive approximations
- Convolution type equations
- Volterra equation of the first kind
- Abel integral equation

#### Fredholm Integral Equations
- Fredholm equations of the second kind
- Method of Fredholm determinants
- Iterated kernels
- Degenerate kernels
- Eigenvalues and eigenfunctions
- Fredholm alternative
- Construction of Green's function for BVP

#### Calculus of Variations
- Euler-Lagrange equations
- Degenerate Euler equations
- Natural boundary conditions, transversality conditions
- Applications of variational principle
- Sufficient conditions for extremum
- Variational formulation of BVP
- Minimum of quadratic functional
- Galerkin's method, weighted-residual methods
- Variational methods for time-dependent problems

- Addition rule, conditional probability

- Bayes' theorem, independence

#### Random Variables

- Mathematical expectation, moments

- Chebyshev's inequality

#### Special Distributions
- Discrete uniform, Binomial, Geometric, Poisson
- Exponential, Gamma, Normal

#### Joint Distributions
- Joint, marginal, conditional distributions
- Product moments, correlation
- Independence of random variables
- Bivariate normal distribution

#### Sampling Distributions
- Central Limit Theorem
- Distributions of sample mean and variance
- Chi-Square, t, F distributions

#### Estimation

- Maximum likelihood estimation

#### Hypothesis Testing

- Critical and acceptance regions, Type I/II errors
- Power of the test

- Shooting methods

- Stability, error, convergence analysis

- Finite difference approximations
- Convergence and stability analysis
- Explicit and Implicit schemes
- Crank-Nicolson scheme, tri-diagonal system
- Laplace equation (five point formulae)
- ADI scheme
- Hyperbolic equation, method of characteristics
- Heat conduction: Schmidt, Crank-Nicolson
- Wave equation
