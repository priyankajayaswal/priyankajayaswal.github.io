🧠 Techniques that actually move the needle

  1. Active recall > re-reading. After each pomo, close the tab and write 3–5 sentences from memory. Grade yourself. 2–3× better retention than highlighting.
  2. Spaced repetition (Anki). Make cards while watching lectures, not later. 10–15 cards / topic / hour. Review 10 min / day — non-negotiable.
  3. Interleaving. Don't batch 5 hrs of one topic. Context-switching across 3 topics / day feels harder but retention is dramatically better (the daily plan already does this).
  4. Feynman technique. Weekly, pick one concept and explain it out loud to an imaginary student. Where you stumble = your real gap.
  5. Problem-first for technical topics. For Compilers, Nuclear, Diff-Geo, Distributed: try a problem before the lecture. Flail for 10 min, then watch. Attention spikes.
  6. Build one artefact per topic. Tiny Raft, SAR image pipeline, parser, JAX matmul, digester sim, Sanskrit sandhi recognizer. Projects turn 20 h of watching into 40 h of knowing.
  7. Teach forward. Push a 1-page cheatsheet per topic to GitHub. Forces compression.
  8. Retrieval weekends. Sundays = no new material. Only quiz yourself on last week's cards + 1 cumulative topic from 4 weeks ago.

🎯 Tactical tweaks for this 15-topic mix

  • Pair related topics on the same day — use Reshuffle to force: Rocket + Nuclear (thermo), Radar + Satellite (EM + sensors), Compilers + PL (type systems + IR), Security-UG + Security-PG.
  • Sanskrit needs daily drip. Language decays fastest — 15 min / day beats 2 h / week.
  • Diff-Geo & TPU benefit hugely from pencil-and-paper derivation. Don't just watch.
  • Biogas & Gold extraction are reading-heavy — play at 1.25× and do weekly Feynman.
  • Distributed Systems & Compilers are lab-driven — schedule 2 full pomos back-to-back (50 min) when coding.

📐 Session structure that works

  1. Minute 0–2: re-read yesterday's 3-sentence recall for this topic.
  2. Minute 2–20: watch / read, pausing every 5 min to predict what comes next.
  3. Minute 20–23: write 3 sentences from memory. Make 2–3 Anki cards.
  4. Minute 23–25: note one question you couldn't answer — becomes tomorrow's opener.

🚩 Warning signs & fixes

  • Same hours, no progress? You're passive-watching. Force recall + projects.
  • Forget last week's topic? Anki frequency too low — raise to 15 min / day.
  • Always picking the same 3 topics? You're in comfort zone. Trust the Reshuffle.
  • Tree keeps dying? Pomo length too long. Drop 50 → 25 until focus stamina grows.
