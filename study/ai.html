$Compute for research — apply, don't pay

Free / heavily subsidised GPU, TPU and HPC time. Most accept independent researchers, students, open‑source maintainers, and small startups — not just credentialed academics. Read the eligibility carefully; turnaround is days to a few weeks.

🛰️Google TPU Research Cloud (TRC)
Free preemptible v2/v3/v4/v5e + on‑demand v5p slices for researchers, in exchange for an open paper/blog/repo. Rolling apps; ~1‑2 wk review.
🟩NVIDIA Academic Hardware Grant
Free A100/H100/RTX 6000 Ada workstations or DGX time for university research. Quarterly cycle.
🚀NVIDIA Inception
Free programme for AI startups — NGC catalogue access, $100k DGX Cloud credits, technical mentorship, GTC tickets.
🅰️AMD AI & HPC Fund (Instinct cluster)
Free remote access to MI210/MI250/MI300X clusters for academic and open‑source projects. Rolling.
🟦Intel Tiber Developer Cloud
Free Gaudi 2 / Gaudi 3, Max GPU and Xeon time for testing. Standard sign‑up; larger allocations via Liftoff program.
🟪Cerebras Fellows / Inference free tier
CS‑3 wafer‑scale time for selected fellows; public free inference API on Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek for everyone.
⚡GroqCloud (free tier)
Generous free LPU inference tier on Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek‑R1 with sub‑100ms TTFT. Sign‑up only.
🧠SambaNova Cloud (free tier)
Free RDU‑backed inference on Llama 405B, DeepSeek‑R1 671B and Qwen. Sign‑up only.
☁️Google Cloud Research Credits
Up to $5,000 (researchers) or $1,000 (students) on GCP — covers A100/H100/TPU. Application‑based.
☁️AWS Cloud Credit for Research
Compute credits and Trainium/Inferentia/H200 access for academic projects. Also: AWS Activate ($1k–$100k) for startups.
